# Adidas-Sales-Analysis
In this file, I conducted an Adidas Sales Analysis using Power BI, evaluating the sales and profit by different categories.

After importing the data, I started looking for answers and results to my questions:

- What is the financial performance of the business? 

In the specified time frame, the Adidas business produced a revenue of $900M, with profit exceeding $300M and 2M units sold.

- Which months drive peak sales throughout the year?

Sales peak in July ($95M), August ($92M), and December($86M), while the lowest period of revenue is in March ($57M).

As for the region, the West region produces the most revenue ($270M), followed by the Northeast ($186M), while at the lowest point sits the Midwest region ($136M)




- Which product line generated the highest sales volume?
The Men’s Street Footwear is the top revenue and sales generator.

Business Recommendations: 

- Invest and expand in the online channels such as Amazon. Growth on such platforms can be achieved by strengthening the digital marketing and direct listing agreements on those digital marketplaces.

- Run a deeper analysis on the different procedures used by West Gear vs Amazon to gather insights on what can be improved.

- Investigate if the small revenue produced by the Midwest area is due to distribution constraints, lower store numbers or brand preference.

- Run targeted promotions and create bundles for the month of March to increase sales.

- Allocate a budget for brand design resources on the lifestyle and streetwear collections.
